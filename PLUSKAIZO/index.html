PLUSKAIZO by +KZ is a difficult game that has a world in the next context: Chydia (the main character) was living in the city, unitl some random day the reality started to get distorted, the city turned into a dangerous forest and a lot of weird monsters appeared. The forest is very dangerous and people can not go through it, however the superpower of Chydia is being the most agile person in the universe, she is the only one that can go through the forest and investigate what is going on.
