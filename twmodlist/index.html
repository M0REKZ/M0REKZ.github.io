Teeworlds archived mod list

Teeworlds is fun by itself with the default gametypes, however, the thing that makes it even more special are the mods made by the community.

Sadly, through the time many of the mods has been lost due to things like dead links or file deletion.

The purpose of this page is to list different Teeworlds mods that existed through the time and are hard to find, while also providing a way to download them by submitting them to Archive.org or Github.

Mods listed here that are still available are included to show the origin for certain forks and showing forks of mods that are unavailable.

(Most of them may be race-related since race mods are the most popular in Teeworlds)

If you think something is missing, feel free to send a pull request.
